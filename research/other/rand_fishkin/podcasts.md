## 1. AI Search and Zero-Click Marketing Explained

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_fKK-ka29Ic

**Publication Date**

23 June 2026

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview with **TopRank B2B Marketing TV**, Rand Fishkin explores how AI-powered search and zero-click behavior are transforming digital marketing. Drawing on SparkToro research, he explains why marketers should shift their focus from maximizing website traffic to building visibility, influence, and trust across the channels where buyers already consume information. The discussion also examines how AI assistants, search engines, and social platforms are reshaping content discovery and marketing measurement.

### Key Insight

Modern B2B marketing should optimize for influence, not just clicks. Companies that publish valuable, platform-native content on LinkedIn while strengthening brand visibility across search, AI assistants, and other discovery channels will be better positioned to build awareness, trust, and long-term demand in the zero-click era.
