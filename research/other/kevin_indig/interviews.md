## 1. Retail SEO Q&A with Kevin Indig

**URL**

**Publication Date**

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this expert Q&A hosted by **SISTRIX**, Kevin Indig discusses modern SEO strategy, search trends, and the evolving relationship between content, user intent, and organic visibility. Drawing from his experience leading SEO and growth at major technology companies, he explains how businesses should approach search strategically rather than relying on outdated optimization tactics. The discussion provides practical insights into measuring performance, adapting to changing search behavior, and building sustainable organic growth.

### Key Insight

Effective organic growth comes from understanding how users discover information across multiple channels, not just search engines. By combining data-driven SEO, high-quality content, and industry expertise, B2B SaaS companies can create authoritative LinkedIn thought leadership that strengthens brand credibility while supporting long-term organic demand generation.
