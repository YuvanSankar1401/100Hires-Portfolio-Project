## 1. Buyer Psychology: Be Buyable, Not Forgettable

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7doL-eq-JCU

**Publication Date**

19 May 2026

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this podcast interview, Katelyn Bourgoin explains how buyer psychology influences brand memorability and purchasing decisions. She discusses why marketers should move beyond feature-based messaging and instead focus on understanding customer motivations, emotional triggers, and decision-making behavior. The conversation provides practical frameworks for creating messaging that resonates with buyers and makes companies easier to remember in competitive B2B SaaS markets.

### Key Insight

The most effective marketing helps companies become memorable before buyers enter the purchasing process. By combining customer research, behavioral psychology, and clear positioning, businesses can create LinkedIn content and educational resources that build familiarity, strengthen trust, and increase the likelihood that prospects think of them when a buying need arises.
