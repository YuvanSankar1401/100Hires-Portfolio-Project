## 1. Rand Fishkin Explains Zero-Click Marketing

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9R_NeY17FU8

**Publication Date**

16 September 2025

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview, Rand Fishkin explains the principles of Zero-Click Marketing and how modern buyer behavior is changing across search engines, AI assistants, and social platforms. He discusses why marketers should focus less on driving immediate clicks and more on building visibility, trust, and brand recognition where audiences already spend their time. The conversation offers practical guidance for adapting B2B SaaS marketing strategies to an increasingly AI-driven discovery environment.

### Key Insight

LinkedIn should be viewed as an influence and brand-building channel rather than simply a source of referral traffic. By consistently publishing valuable educational content directly on the platform, companies can increase visibility, strengthen brand recall, and influence buying decisions long before prospects visit their website.
