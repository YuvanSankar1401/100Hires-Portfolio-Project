### Why This Resource Is Valuable

The Exit Five Newsletter is Dave Gerhardt's flagship publication for B2B marketers, delivering practical insights on brand building, demand generation, content marketing, AI, leadership, and career growth. Drawing on interviews with experienced marketing leaders and real-world case studies, the newsletter focuses on actionable strategies rather than theory, making it a valuable resource for B2B SaaS teams seeking repeatable marketing frameworks.

### Key Insight

An owned newsletter transforms social attention into a long-term audience relationship. By sharing valuable insights consistently on LinkedIn and expanding those ideas through a newsletter, marketers can build trust, strengthen their brand, and create a sustainable channel that supports demand generation beyond changing social media algorithms.
