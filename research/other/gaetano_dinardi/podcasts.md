## 1. The State of Demand Gen 2021 with Gaetano DiNardi

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-kH8_61Bjsk

**Publication Date**

17 July 2021

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this conversation with Chris Walker, Gaetano DiNardi discusses the evolution of B2B demand generation, the changing role of content marketing, and the importance of aligning marketing activities with revenue outcomes. Drawing on his experience leading growth at SaaS companies, he explains why successful demand generation requires a balance of brand building, educational content, SEO, and customer-focused marketing rather than relying solely on lead-generation tactics.

### Key Insight

Demand generation is most effective when marketing creates trust before buyers enter the sales process. Consistent educational content, strong positioning, and measurable business impact help organizations build sustainable pipeline growth. These principles make LinkedIn an essential channel for sharing expertise, strengthening brand authority, and supporting long-term B2B SaaS demand generation.
