## 1. SEO and Content Marketing in the Age of AI

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLpVV0sQrfg

**Publication Date**

25 August 2025

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this podcast interview, Kevin Indig discusses how artificial intelligence is transforming SEO, content marketing, and organic growth. He explains how AI-powered search experiences are changing user behavior, why traditional SEO metrics are becoming less reliable, and how B2B SaaS companies should adapt their content strategies to remain discoverable. The conversation offers practical guidance on combining data analysis, strategic content creation, and AI-aware optimization to build sustainable organic growth.

### Key Insight

Content teams must optimize for modern discovery rather than traditional search rankings alone. By creating authoritative content that answers user intent, strengthens brand credibility, and performs across search engines, AI assistants, and LinkedIn, B2B SaaS companies can maintain visibility and generate long-term organic demand in the evolving AI search landscape.
