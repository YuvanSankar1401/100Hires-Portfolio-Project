### Why This Resource Is Valuable

April Dunford's official website serves as the central hub for her books, blog articles, newsletter, podcast, positioning templates, consulting services, and educational resources. It brings together her battle-tested positioning methodology developed through years of working with B2B technology companies and startups. The site provides practical guidance for founders, product marketers, and sales teams looking to strengthen product positioning and go-to-market strategy.

### Key Insight

Effective positioning determines how customers understand a product's value before they ever evaluate its features. By clearly defining differentiated capabilities, ideal customers, competitive alternatives, and market category, companies create stronger messaging, more effective marketing, and better sales conversations.
