## 1. Buyer's Psychology with Katelyn Bourgoin

**URL**

**Publication Date**

13 October 2022

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview with **Demand Curve**, Katelyn Bourgoin explains how buyer psychology influences purchasing decisions and why effective marketing begins with understanding customer motivations rather than product features. She discusses practical approaches to customer interviews, identifying buying triggers, uncovering customer language, and applying behavioral science to improve messaging and positioning. The conversation provides actionable frameworks that B2B SaaS marketers can use to create content that resonates with real buyer needs.

### Key Insight

Customer research is one of the highest-leverage activities in B2B marketing. By understanding why customers buy, the problems they are trying to solve, and the language they naturally use, companies can produce LinkedIn content, messaging, and positioning that feels relevant, builds trust, and increases the likelihood of meaningful customer engagement.
