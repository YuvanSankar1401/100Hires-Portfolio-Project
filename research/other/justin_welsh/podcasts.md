## 1. The Solopreneur: He Makes $1.7M With 0 Employees

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6IF-lVuvbs

**Publication Date**

24 April 2023

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this long-form podcast interview, Justin Welsh explains how he built a highly profitable one-person business through consistent content creation, audience building, digital products, and operational simplicity. He shares practical lessons on creating leverage, developing repeatable business systems, and using LinkedIn as the primary platform for establishing authority and attracting qualified customers. The conversation provides valuable insights for professionals looking to build sustainable creator-led businesses without large teams or complex operations.

### Key Insight

A high-trust audience is one of the most valuable business assets. By consistently sharing educational content on LinkedIn, nurturing relationships through an email newsletter, and offering products that solve specific customer problems, creators can build predictable revenue streams while keeping their business simple, scalable, and independent of paid acquisition.
