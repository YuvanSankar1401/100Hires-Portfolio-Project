## 1. Hiring Advice – Peep Laja – Wynter

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_DaGAtlHAjc

**Publication Date**

24 July 2021

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview with **Masters of SaaS (Upraw Media)**, Peep Laja discusses hiring high-performing marketers, building effective B2B marketing teams, and creating organizations that consistently execute strong messaging and growth strategies. Drawing from his experience as the founder of Wynter, he explains why successful marketing depends on hiring people who understand customer research, positioning, and evidence-based decision-making rather than relying on intuition alone.

### Key Insight

Strong marketing is the result of both great messaging and capable teams. Organizations that invest in skilled marketers, customer research, and continuous message validation are better positioned to create effective LinkedIn content, improve demand generation, and build sustainable competitive advantages.
