## 1. What Still Works? Content, Search, and Distribution in the AI Era

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=du26Ywa-CtA

**Publication Date**

19 June 2026

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview with **Writesonic**, Ross Simmonds explores how AI is transforming content marketing, search, and digital distribution. He explains why simply producing more AI-generated content is no longer a competitive advantage and argues that successful brands will differentiate themselves through strategic distribution, audience engagement, and original insights. The discussion offers practical guidance for B2B SaaS marketers on adapting content strategies to an AI-driven marketing landscape.

### Key Insight

As AI makes content creation faster and more accessible, distribution becomes the true competitive advantage. Companies that consistently repurpose valuable content, publish platform-native insights on LinkedIn, and build repeatable distribution systems will achieve greater visibility, stronger brand authority, and more sustainable demand generation than those focused solely on content production.
