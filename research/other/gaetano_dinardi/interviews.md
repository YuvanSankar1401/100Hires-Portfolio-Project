## 1. The Secret to Hiring Great Marketers and Doubling Your MQLs with Gaetano DiNardi

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZggIV3_dah0

**Publication Date**

5 November 2021

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview with *Learn & Grow with SmartBug*, Gaetano DiNardi shares his approach to building high-performing B2B marketing teams, improving demand generation, and scaling marketing operations. Drawing from his experience leading growth at SaaS companies, he explains how hiring the right marketers, aligning content with business objectives, and measuring performance beyond vanity metrics contribute to sustainable pipeline growth.

### Key Insight

Successful demand generation depends on combining skilled marketing talent with a clear strategic direction. Organizations that build teams capable of producing consistent, high-quality content, optimizing for business outcomes, and continuously learning from customer data create stronger marketing engines that drive qualified leads, revenue growth, and long-term competitive advantage.
