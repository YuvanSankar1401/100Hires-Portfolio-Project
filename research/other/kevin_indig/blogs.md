### Why This Resource Is Valuable

Kevin Indig's official website is the central hub for his research, essays, newsletter, and advisory work on SEO, AI search, organic growth, product-led growth, and content strategy. Drawing on his experience leading SEO and growth at companies such as Shopify and G2, Kevin publishes data-driven analyses that help B2B SaaS companies understand how search behavior, AI, and content discovery are evolving. His work emphasizes strategic thinking backed by research rather than relying on outdated SEO tactics.

### Key Insight

Sustainable organic growth begins with structured analysis rather than assumptions about channels. By combining data, customer behavior, competitive research, and experimentation, companies can develop content strategies that improve visibility across traditional search, AI-powered discovery, and LinkedIn thought leadership while adapting to changing market dynamics.
