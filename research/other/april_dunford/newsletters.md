### Why This Resource Is Valuable

April Dunford's official newsletter delivers practical insights on product positioning, messaging, category creation, and go-to-market strategy for B2B SaaS companies. Drawing from her consulting experience with technology startups and enterprise software businesses, the newsletter provides actionable frameworks, real-world positioning examples, and strategic advice for founders, product marketers, and sales leaders.

### Key Insight

Consistent positioning education helps organizations communicate a clear and differentiated point of view across every customer touchpoint. By regularly sharing positioning insights through newsletters and LinkedIn thought leadership, companies reinforce their market category, strengthen brand authority, and make it easier for buyers to understand why they are different from competitors.
