## 1. How This B2B Marketer Grew a Company to $20M Starting From Zero

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A-lIwMUUlx4

**Publication Date**

12 March 2026

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview on *The Dave Gerhardt Show*, Dave speaks with a successful B2B marketing operator about building a company from zero to $20 million in revenue. The discussion explores how strong branding, audience building, community engagement, and consistent content creation contribute to sustainable business growth. Rather than focusing solely on paid acquisition, the conversation highlights the long-term value of trust, executive thought leadership, and demand generation.

### Key Insight

Sustainable B2B growth comes from building a recognizable brand that consistently educates and earns customer trust. Companies that invest in executive content, authentic storytelling, and audience-first marketing create durable demand engines that continue generating opportunities long after individual campaigns end.
