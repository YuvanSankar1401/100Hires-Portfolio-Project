## 1. How Justin Welsh Built an $8M Business (With 0 Employees)

**URL**

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IeEF7oKd30M

**Publication Date**

25 November 2024

### Why This Resource Is Valuable

In this interview, Justin Welsh explains the operating principles behind building an $8 million one-person business without employees. He discusses how consistent content creation, audience-first thinking, productized expertise, and simple business systems enable sustainable growth without relying on a large team. The conversation offers practical insights into leveraging LinkedIn, newsletters, and digital products to build a scalable creator business.

### Key Insight

Long-term business growth comes from building repeatable content systems rather than chasing viral moments. By consistently publishing valuable educational content, nurturing an owned audience, and creating products that solve specific customer problems, professionals can establish authority, generate predictable demand, and build a resilient business with minimal operational complexity.
